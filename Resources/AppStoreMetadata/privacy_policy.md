# Privacy Policy — Oriental Instrument

**Effective Date**: June 2026

## Overview

Oriental Instrument ("App") is developed by DJBILBOX BEATS. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Data Collection

### Collected Data
1. **Audio Input** (if microphone permission granted)
   - Local processing only
   - NOT sent to servers
   - Used for real-time audio monitoring/display

2. **IAP & Purchases** (via Apple/Google)
   - Handled by App Store / Play Store
   - We do NOT store payment details
   - Receipt validation only on-device

3. **Device Info** (non-identifying)
   - iOS version, Device model
   - Used for crash reporting (optional)
   - Crash logs: NOT sent without explicit opt-in

4. **User Presets** (optional cloud sync)
   - Stored locally on device by default
   - Cloud save: OFF by default, requires explicit opt-in
   - If enabled: iCloud (iOS) / Google Drive (Android) — encrypted, your account

### NOT Collected
- Location data
- Contacts or call history
- Camera access (not requested)
- Photos or files (except presets you explicitly save)
- Behavioral tracking / analytics

## Data Usage

- **Audio**: Real-time synthesis — never stored or transmitted
- **Presets**: Local file storage; optional cloud backup (your iCloud/Drive account)
- **Crashes**: Diagnostic logs sent ONLY if you opt-in
- **Marketing**: No personal data shared with third parties

## Third Parties

- **Apple App Store**: Handles IAP, crash reporting (optional)
- **Google Play Store**: Handles IAP, crash reporting (optional)
- **iCloud / Google Drive**: Only if you enable cloud preset sync
- **No ad networks or tracking services**

## Your Rights

- **Delete data**: Remove presets and app data via device settings
- **Disable cloud sync**: Available in app preferences
- **Opt out of crashes**: Turn off diagnostic reporting in iOS/Android settings
