Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Afrotrap production different from standard trap? +

Afrotrap combines trap's rhythmic foundation (808s, hi-hats, snares) with authentic African percussion, scales, and cultural sensibilities. Key differences include African polyrhythmic traditions (3-against-4, 5-against-4), use of African scales with micro-tonal inflections, authentic percussion samples (talking drums, kora, balafon), and cultural authenticity rather than appropriation.

Standard trap producers without this cultural foundation often create beats that sound "African-inspired" but lack depth. Authentic Afrotrap requires cultural knowledge + production skill.
